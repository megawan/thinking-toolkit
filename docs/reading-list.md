# Reading list

Sources behind the methods and good places to go deeper. Check publication details in your library or bookshop before citing.

| --- | --- |

| Six Thinking Hats | Edward de Bono, *Six Thinking Hats* (1985) |
| Five Whys | Taiichi Ohno, *Toyota Production System: Beyond Large-Scale Production* (English edition 1988) |
| Fishbone Diagram | Kaoru Ishikawa, *Guide to Quality Control* |
| Pareto Analysis | Joseph M. Juran, *Quality Control Handbook* (first edition 1951); Richard Koch, *The 80/20 Principle* (1997) |
| SCAMPER | Bob Eberle, *SCAMPER: Games for Imagination Development* (1971) |

## General reading

- Daniel Kahneman, *Thinking, Fast and Slow* (2011): how intuition and deliberate thought each go wrong
- Chip Heath and Dan Heath, *Decisive* (2013): a practical process for better decisions
- Donella H. Meadows, *Thinking in Systems* (2008): cause and effect in connected systems
- Michael Michalko, *Thinkertoys* (1991): a large collection of creativity techniques
- Gary Klein, *Sources of Power* (1998): how experts decide under pressure

## Where to look next

New methods the toolkit may add include systems thinking, second-order thinking, Fermi estimation and Bayesian updating. The books above cover several of these.
