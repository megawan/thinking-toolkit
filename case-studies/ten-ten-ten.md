# Case study: Replying to a harsh email

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [10-10-10](../methods/decision-making/ten-ten-ten.md)

## Context

A team lead received a sharp, unfair email from a colleague late in the evening and wanted to reply at once.

## What was done

- She compared three options: an angry reply, waiting until the next day, or asking for a short call
- In 10 minutes the angry reply felt best; in 10 months it risked a strained working relationship; in 10 years it hardly mattered, except as a record of poor conduct
- The call option looked best on the longer horizons

## Outcome

She sent a brief acknowledgement that night and asked for a call the next day. The misunderstanding was cleared up and the working relationship stayed intact.

## Lessons

- The 10-month view was the most useful and the one she would have skipped
- The method took five minutes
- The feeling in the first 10 minutes was real but not decisive
