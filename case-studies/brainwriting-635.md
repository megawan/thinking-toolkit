# Case study: Cutting disposable cups in an office kitchen

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [Brainwriting 6-3-5](../methods/ideation/brainwriting-635.md)

## Context

A 60-person office threw away hundreds of paper cups a week. Earlier meetings on the subject were dominated by two enthusiastic people, and most colleagues said nothing.

## What was done

- Six volunteers ran a 6-3-5 session on the question 'How might we reduce disposable cups in the kitchen?'
- Each wrote three ideas per round and built on the sheet they received
- After six rounds, the group had about ninety ideas
- Ideas were clustered into five themes: make reusables easy, make disposables harder to reach, track results, rewards and awareness

## Outcome

The team voted and tested two ideas for a month: personalised mugs on hooks and a weekly leaderboard. Disposable cup use fell noticeably during the trial.

## Lessons

- Quiet colleagues produced several of the ideas that were chosen
- Building on earlier ideas created combinations nobody wrote first
- Most of the value came from the clustering step
