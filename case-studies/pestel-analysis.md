# Case study: Opening a delivery-only restaurant

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [PESTEL Analysis](../methods/strategy-and-risk/pestel-analysis.md)

## Context

Two partners planned to open a delivery-only restaurant in a city and had focused only on menu and price.

## What was done

- They scanned the six factors: a city plan to limit delivery vehicles in the centre (political), rising food and rent costs (economic), more people working from home and ordering lunch (social), platform commissions of 25 to 30 percent (technological), expectations of less plastic packaging (environmental) and kitchen licensing and courier labour rules (legal)
- They rated licensing, commissions, costs and the home-working trend as high priority

## Outcome

The partners chose a location outside the restricted zone, built direct ordering to reduce commission, planned recyclable packaging as a selling point, and checked licensing and courier contracts before signing a lease.

## Lessons

- The legal and political factors changed the choice of location
- Each high-priority factor led to a concrete action
- Indicators were set to review the scan regularly
