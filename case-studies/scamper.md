# Case study: A coffee shop with empty afternoons

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [SCAMPER](../methods/ideation/scamper.md)

## Context

A neighbourhood coffee shop had a busy morning rush but a nearly empty room from 2 pm to 6 pm, while rent and staff costs stayed the same.

## What was done

- Substitute: iced drinks and tea in the afternoon
- Combine: coffee with a quiet co-working corner
- Adapt: a 'happy hour' discount borrowed from bars
- Eliminate: table service in the afternoon
- Reverse: deliver coffee pots to nearby offices

## Outcome

Three ideas were tried for one month: afternoon discount hours, a co-working corner with power sockets, and office pot delivery. Afternoon sales improved most from the co-working corner and delivery; the discount alone helped less.

## Lessons

- Seven narrow questions produced more options than open brainstorming
- Testing three ideas cheaply was better than choosing one up front
- The core purpose (use the room and equipment) stayed in view
