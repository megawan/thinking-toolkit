# Case study: A four-day work week proposal

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [Six Thinking Hats](../methods/decision-making/six-thinking-hats.md)

## Context

A 25-person agency was split on trying a four-day week. Earlier discussions ended in opinions about whether it was 'realistic'.

## What was done

- White: output steady for two quarters; two clients need Friday availability; a survey showed most staff in favour
- Red: staff excited, managers uneasy about control
- Black: response times and deadlines could suffer
- Yellow: retention and hiring appeal
- Green: rotate days off, pilot for three months, rotating Friday support

## Outcome

The group agreed on a three-month pilot with a rotating Friday support team and two metrics to review in month three.

## Lessons

- Separating feelings from facts made the unease discussable
- The Green hat turned a yes/no into a pilot
- Blue closed with an owner and a review date
