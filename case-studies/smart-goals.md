# Case study: Making documentation better

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [SMART Goals](../methods/planning/smart-goals.md)

## Context

A team wanted to 'make our documentation better'. A year earlier the same goal had been set and nothing measurable had changed.

## What was done

- Specific: the setup and troubleshooting guides, not all documentation
- Measurable: setup-related support tickets fall by 30 percent
- Achievable: two writers, two days a week
- Relevant: setup was the largest ticket category
- Time-bound: guides rewritten by 30 June, result measured at the end of the quarter

## Outcome

The goal was written as one sentence and put on the team board. The guides were rewritten on time, and setup tickets fell by roughly a quarter in the quarter, close to the target. The team adjusted the next goal based on what was left.

## Lessons

- The relevance check picked the right guides to improve
- A measure made the result visible, even when the target was not met exactly
- A review date turned a wish into a routine
