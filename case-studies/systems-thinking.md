# Case study: A support team that never catches up

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [Systems Thinking](../methods/mental-models/systems-thinking.md)

## Context

A support team worked overtime for months yet the queue kept growing, and hiring requests did not seem to help.

## What was done

- The team drew a reinforcing loop: more workload led to overtime, then fatigue and errors, then rework, then more workload
- They drew a balancing loop with a delay: hiring pressure led to new hires, which reduced workload, but training them first added work for several months
- Overtime relieved the queue this week and strengthened the rework loop

## Outcome

The team added a short checklist and training to cut rework, and began hiring earlier to shorten the delay. The queue shrank over the next quarter.

## Lessons

- The diagram explained why the usual fix failed
- A delay in the loop was the reason hiring seemed not to work
- The change targeted the loop, not the symptom
