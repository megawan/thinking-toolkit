# Case study: A first-time home buyer

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [Empathy Map](../methods/design-and-product/empathy-map.md)

## Context

A mortgage start-up interviewed first-time buyers and found that its team described them as 'price-driven', which did not match the interviews.

## What was done

- The team built an empathy map from eight interviews
- Says: 'I just want to know if I can afford it.' Does: used three calculators and a spreadsheet and avoided phone calls with banks. Feels: anxious about money, embarrassed about not knowing terms
- Tension: they said they wanted to know if they could afford it but avoided contacting banks

## Outcome

The team built an early, private, jargon-free affordability check with no commitment and no phone call. More visitors completed it than completed the old enquiry form.

## Lessons

- The gap between what people said and what they did produced the insight
- Marking inferences stopped the team treating guesses as facts
- One map per segment kept the picture clear
