# Case study: A report that arrived two days late

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [Ladder of Inference](../methods/critical-thinking/ladder-of-inference.md)

## Context

A team lead's colleague delivered a report two days late and sent no message. The lead was about to stop giving that colleague important work.

## What was done

- Observable data: the report arrived Thursday instead of Tuesday and there was no message
- The lead had also heard that the colleague was working on a client emergency, but had not connected the two facts
- Her first interpretation was 'they don't care about deadlines'; alternatives were that they were stretched and did not know how to raise it
- She asked: 'The report came on Thursday and I expected Tuesday. Something may have got in the way. What happened?'

## Outcome

The colleague explained the client emergency, and they agreed on how to flag problems early. The lead kept giving them important work.

## Lessons

- Walking down the ladder separated what she saw from what she made of it
- A question replaced a verdict
- The conclusion changed once the unselected data was included
