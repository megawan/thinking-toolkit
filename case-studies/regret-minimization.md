# Case study: Leaving a stable job to start a design studio

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [Regret Minimization](../methods/decision-making/regret-minimization.md)

## Context

An engineer in a stable job had wanted to run a small design studio for years. Pay, benefits and family obligations made staying the safe choice.

## What was done

- She compared the regret of failing (money stress, going back to a similar job after a year) with the regret of never trying
- She judged the worst case survivable
- She limited the risk by saving six months of costs and signing two clients before resigning

## Outcome

She left with a plan and a safety margin. The first year was hard, but she kept the studio and did not feel she had avoided the question.

## Lessons

- The method clarified what she valued; it did not remove the risk
- Limiting the downside made the leap realistic
- Obligations to family were included in the regret check
