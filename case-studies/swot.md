# Case study: A bakery considering online delivery

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [SWOT Analysis](../methods/strategy-and-risk/swot.md)

## Context

A small bakery with loyal local customers wanted to start delivery, but had no vehicle, a small kitchen and no website.

## What was done

- Strengths: loyal customers, strong sourdough reputation. Weaknesses: no vehicle, limited capacity
- Opportunities: nearby offices with no bakery, demand for subscriptions. Threats: supermarket bread, delivery-app fees of 25-30 percent
- TOWS pairing produced four actions

## Outcome

The bakery started with a weekly sourdough subscription for nearby offices and pickup points, set a daily order cap to protect the kitchen, and postponed buying a vehicle.

## Lessons

- Pairing the quadrants produced the plan; the lists alone did not
- Weaknesses shaped the first step (pickup, not vehicles)
- A review date kept the SWOT from going stale
