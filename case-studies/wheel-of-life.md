# Case study: Taking stock after a busy year

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [Wheel of Life](../methods/reflection-and-learning/wheel-of-life.md)

## Context

A project lead had worked long hours for a year, felt tired and could not say which part of her life needed attention.

## What was done

- She scored eight areas: career 8, finances 6, health 3, family and friends 5, partner 7, personal growth 5, fun and recreation 3, environment 7
- She saw that work was strong but health and fun were low, and that poor health affected her energy at work
- She chose two focus areas with small actions: a 30-minute walk three days a week and bed by 23:00 on weeknights; one social or hobby evening every two weeks

## Outcome

After three months health and fun had each risen from 3 to 5 while work stayed at 8. She repeated the wheel and chose personal growth next.

## Lessons

- The picture made the pattern obvious
- Two small actions were realistic; eight would not have been
- Repeating the wheel showed real change
