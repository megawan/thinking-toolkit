# Case study: Customer support tickets

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [Pareto Analysis](../methods/prioritisation/pareto-analysis.md)

## Context

A support team of five received about 500 tickets a month and felt overwhelmed. Every category seemed equally urgent.

## What was done

- Tickets from one month were grouped into nine categories
- Three categories (password reset, billing errors, login failures) accounted for about three quarters of volume
- The largest, password reset, was over a third by itself

## Outcome

The team added self-service password reset, fixed the billing export that caused errors and reviewed the login flow. The next month's count fell and the ranking shifted, so they re-ran the analysis.

## Lessons

- Counting turned a general feeling into a ranked list
- Rankings change once the top item is fixed
- Pareto showed where, not why; the cause still needed investigation
