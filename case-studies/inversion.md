# Case study: Planning a product launch

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [Inversion](../methods/mental-models/inversion.md)

## Context

A team wrote a launch plan full of positive steps, and it still felt vague.

## What was done

- They asked 'how would we guarantee a failed launch?'
- The list: launch without talking to customers, set the date by wish, skip testing, leave support untrained, change scope weekly
- They compared the list with current behaviour and found that scope had changed every week

## Outcome

Each item became a rule: customer interviews before locking scope, a date from an estimate with buffer, a scope freeze two weeks before launch, support training, and a staged rollout.

## Lessons

- Comparing the list with current behaviour was the most useful step
- Specific failure items turned directly into rules
- Positive steps were still added alongside
