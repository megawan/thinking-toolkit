# Case study: A meal-planning app for busy parents

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [Value Proposition Canvas](../methods/design-and-product/value-proposition-canvas.md)

## Context

A small team planned a meal-planning app, and its list of features was growing without a clear link to what parents wanted.

## What was done

- Customer profile: jobs were feeding the family healthy weekday meals, avoiding arguments at dinner and controlling the food budget; pains were deciding what to cook, wasting food and forgetting items; gains were a weekly plan in under ten minutes and children eating without complaint
- Value map: a one-tap weekly plan, a generated shopping list and reuse of leftover ingredients
- Fit check: 'children eating without complaint' was only partly covered

## Outcome

The team cut two features that addressed no important pain, and interviewed ten parents about what children would eat. A 'family favourites' feature was tested next.

## Lessons

- Linking each feature to a pain or gain removed waste
- The fit check pointed to the next research question
- Ranking limited the work to the few things that decide the choice
