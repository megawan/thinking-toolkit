# Case study: Launching a mobile app

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [Pre-mortem](../methods/strategy-and-risk/pre-mortem.md)

## Context

A product team of eight, confident about a launch in four months, held a pre-mortem at kickoff.

## What was done

- Each member wrote reasons for failure silently, then shared them one at a time
- Themes: delivery (only one developer knew the payment code), market (no clear reason to switch), technical (slow on older phones), operations (no support owner)
- Market and operations were rated high likelihood

## Outcome

The team paired a second developer on payments, ran ten user interviews to test the reason to switch before building further, tested on older phones, and named a support owner.

## Lessons

- Quiet team members raised the most specific risks
- Rating likelihood and impact kept the list manageable
- Every risk left with an owner
