# Case study: A restaurant's mobile ordering page

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [Crazy 8s](../methods/ideation/crazy-8s.md)

## Context

A neighbourhood restaurant's online takeaway page was hard to use on a phone, and many customers still ordered by calling.

## What was done

- The owner and two staff members each did a Crazy 8s round on 'How might we make ordering easy on a phone?'
- Ideas ranged from a bigger button, sticky categories and photo-first menus to chat-style ordering, one-tap reorder and a barcode on the takeaway bag
- Each person shared their sheet in a minute, then everyone placed three dots on their favourites

## Outcome

One-tap reorder, sticky categories and a three-step order were combined into a design that was tested with five regular customers.

## Lessons

- The strongest idea appeared in the later panels, not the first
- Rough sketches made it easy to drop weak ideas
- Combining parts of different sketches beat choosing one
