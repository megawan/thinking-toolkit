# Case study: Launching an online course

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [Backcasting](../methods/planning/backcasting.md)

## Context

A teacher wanted to launch a paid online course with 100 students by 1 September. Starting from zero audience and no content in March, forward planning kept slipping.

## What was done

- She worked backwards: marketing and a waiting list of 300 in August, beta test with 15 students in July, lessons recorded by June, scripts in May, outline validated with 10 interviews in April
- The gap analysis showed that the biggest jump was building an audience from zero
- She added a weekly newsletter from April so the audience would grow during production

## Outcome

The first step was clear: book the interviews that week. The plan gave her a monthly checklist, and she moved the launch date by two weeks when the beta feedback required changes to the lessons.

## Lessons

- The gap analysis found the real risk (audience) that a forward plan had hidden
- Dated milestones made it obvious when to adjust
- A first step this week turned the plan into action
