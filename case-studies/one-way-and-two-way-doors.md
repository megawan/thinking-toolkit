# Case study: Buying a machine for a new product line

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [One-way and Two-way Doors](../methods/decision-making/one-way-and-two-way-doors.md)

## Context

A small manufacturer wanted to buy a $30,000 machine for a product line that had not been tested in the market.

## What was done

- The team classified the purchase as a one-way door: money spent, low resale value
- They looked for ways to make it reversible: rent for three months, buy used, or outsource a first batch
- They chose to outsource a first batch and rent the machine for the follow-up run

## Outcome

For a small cost the team learned that demand was lower than hoped and redesigned the product before buying anything.

## Lessons

- Asking what it takes to undo a decision revealed cheaper ways to learn
- The door metaphor shortened the meeting
- A review date was set before spending more
