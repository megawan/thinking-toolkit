# Case study: A neighbourhood library with falling visits

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [How Might We](../methods/ideation/how-might-we.md)

## Context

A neighbourhood library saw visits drop by about a third, especially among young adults. Staff blamed the internet and lack of funding.

## What was done

- The team listed insights: young adults want a quiet place with power and Wi-Fi, think the library is for children, and do not know about events
- They wrote more than twenty HMW questions and removed those with hidden solutions, such as 'How might we build an app?'
- They chose two: 'How might we make the library the best study spot in the area?' and 'How might we show young adults that the library is for them?'

## Outcome

The chosen questions went into a brainwriting session. Ideas included extended study hours with power outlets, a young-adult advisory group and events posted where young adults already spend time.

## Lessons

- Insights came first; the questions followed from them
- Removing hidden solutions kept the session open
- The reframing moved the team from blame to options
