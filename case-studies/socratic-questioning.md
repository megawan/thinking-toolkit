# Case study: Reviewing a plan for a second shop

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [Socratic Questioning](../methods/critical-thinking/socratic-questioning.md)

## Context

A shop owner was set on opening a second shop in a nearby neighbourhood and asked a friend for feedback.

## What was done

- The friend asked what success would look like (half the first shop's sales in a year) and what the owner was assuming (the same customer mix)
- Asked how she knew, the owner admitted she had not checked; asked how a competitor would respond, she realised they might cut prices
- Asked whether opening a second shop was the real question, she said it might be growing sales

## Outcome

The owner decided to test the new neighbourhood with a market stall for a month and to look at online sales first.

## Lessons

- Questions surfaced assumptions the owner had not examined
- The last question reframed the problem
- The owner reached the conclusion herself and acted on it
