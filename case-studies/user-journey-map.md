# Case study: Signing up for a gym membership

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [User Journey Map](../methods/design-and-product/user-journey-map.md)

## Context

A gym had a good number of enquiries but many people did not complete joining or stopped after a few weeks. Each department thought its own part worked well.

## What was done

- A cross-functional team mapped the journey of a new member in five stages: discover, visit, join, first week and month three
- The lowest points were joining (a paper form with repeated data entry) and the first week (the booking app crashing)
- Another low was cancelling, which required a visit

## Outcome

The team prioritised a short online form with e-signature, a fix to the booking flow with a welcome email, and online pause and cancel with a check-in message. Completed sign-ups rose and early drop-off fell.

## Lessons

- Seeing the whole journey exposed handoffs no department owned
- The emotion line made the low points easy to agree on
- Opportunities were prioritised and given owners
