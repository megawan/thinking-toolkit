# Case study: Late deliveries at a regional online shop

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [Fishbone Diagram](../methods/root-cause/fishbone.md)

## Context

An online shop promised delivery in two days, but about one order in four arrived late. Individual explanations (couriers, weather) kept being offered, and none explained the pattern.

## What was done

- The team of six built a fishbone with six categories in one hour
- Process stood out: orders were batched only twice a day and there was no address check
- People: Monday shifts were understaffed
- They pulled three weeks of order records to test the process causes first

## Outcome

Most late orders were placed after the second batch cut-off, and many had address errors. A third daily batch, address validation at checkout and delivery promises based on real cut-off times cut late orders sharply in the following month.

## Lessons

- The diagram prevented fixating on the courier
- Evidence, not the diagram, ranked the causes
- Testing two candidates first saved effort on the rest
