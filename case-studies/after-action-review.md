# Case study: A customer webinar

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [After Action Review](../methods/reflection-and-learning/after-action-review.md)

## Context

A software company ran a customer webinar and felt it had gone 'okay', without being able to say what to repeat or change.

## What was done

- Within a day, the five people involved held a 20-minute review
- Expected: 300 registrations, 150 live attendees and a smooth demo. Actual: 280 registrations, 90 attendees and a demo that froze for two minutes
- Reasons: reminder emails went out only the night before, the demo ran on a laptop with a weak connection, and the best-received part was a customer story
- Actions: two reminder emails, a recorded backup demo and a customer story at the start

## Outcome

At the next webinar attendance rose and the demo ran from a recorded fallback. The customer story stayed at the start of the agenda.

## Lessons

- Comparing the plan with the result gave a neutral starting point
- Reviewing what went well kept the best part
- Doing it within a day kept the details accurate
