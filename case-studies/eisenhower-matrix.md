# Case study: A project manager's overloaded Monday

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [Eisenhower Matrix](../methods/prioritisation/eisenhower-matrix.md)

## Context

A project manager arrived to 23 open items and answered whatever arrived last. By Friday the quarterly roadmap, due soon, had not been touched.

## What was done

- All items were listed and rated against two goals: ship the release, and keep the roadmap on schedule
- A broken payment page and a Wednesday proposal went to quadrant 1
- The roadmap and learning a new reporting tool went to quadrant 2 with fixed calendar slots
- A vendor invoice query and an optional status call went to quadrant 3 (delegate or decline)
- Folder tidying went to quadrant 4 and was dropped

## Outcome

Quadrant 1 was finished by Tuesday, and the roadmap got two protected two-hour blocks that week. Fewer items stayed open at the end of the week.

## Lessons

- The calendar slot, not the label, protected quadrant 2
- Declining quadrant 3 items needed a polite standard reply
- Re-sorting weekly caught items drifting into quadrant 1
