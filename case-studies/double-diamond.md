# Case study: Reducing missed medical appointments

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [Double Diamond](../methods/design-and-product/double-diamond.md)

## Context

A clinic had many missed appointments and planned to introduce text reminders because it assumed patients forgot.

## What was done

- Discover: the team interviewed 15 patients who missed appointments, reviewed booking data and shadowed reception
- Define: many could not attend and found it hard to tell the clinic, so slots were wasted
- Develop: ideas included one-tap cancel by text, a waiting list, a phone call back and a travel voucher; the text flow was prototyped
- Deliver: the text flow was tested for a month in one clinic, the wording refined, and it was rolled out

## Outcome

Missed appointments fell, and freed slots were offered to a waiting list. The reminder system the team first planned would not have addressed the real problem.

## Lessons

- The first diamond changed the problem from forgetting to difficulty cancelling
- Prototyping several options kept the second diamond open
- A one-clinic test limited the risk of the rollout
