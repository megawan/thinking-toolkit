# Case study: A neighbourhood repair cafe

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [Business Model and Lean Canvas](../methods/strategy-and-risk/business-model-and-lean-canvas.md)

## Context

A group of residents wanted to start a monthly repair cafe where volunteers fix household items, but had no plan for money, people or venue.

## What was done

- They filled a Lean Canvas: older residents with small appliances as the first segment, the problem that repairs cost more than replacement, a monthly repair day with volunteer repairers as the solution, and donations plus small parts fees as revenue
- The riskiest assumption was that enough skilled volunteers would come every month
- They asked 20 local tradespeople and hobbyists to commit to one session each before booking a venue

## Outcome

Fourteen people committed, enough for the first six sessions. The group booked a venue and updated the canvas with the first measured numbers.

## Lessons

- The canvas showed the volunteer assumption as the riskiest, so it was tested before spending money
- Short phrases kept the page readable
- The canvas was updated after the first test
