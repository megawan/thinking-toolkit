# Case study: Reviewing a subscription product launch

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [Red Team and Devil's Advocate](../methods/critical-thinking/red-team-and-devils-advocate.md)

## Context

A product team planned to launch a subscription in three markets in two months. Everyone on the team was enthusiastic.

## What was done

- A colleague from another team was asked to argue against the plan
- Findings: the launch depended on a payment partner not yet confirmed, pricing was tested in only one segment, and support was not staffed for three time zones
- The owners recorded each finding with a response

## Outcome

The team confirmed the partner in writing, ran a second price test, and phased the launch: one market first and the others when support was ready.

## Lessons

- An outsider found issues the team had stopped seeing
- Written responses made it harder to ignore findings
- The review happened early enough for changes to be cheap
