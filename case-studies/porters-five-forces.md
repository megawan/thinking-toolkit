# Case study: An independent online bookshop

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [Porter's Five Forces](../methods/strategy-and-risk/porters-five-forces.md)

## Context

A small online bookshop was losing sales to large platforms and wondered where it could realistically compete.

## What was done

- The owner rated new entrants as high (setting up is cheap), buyers high (customers compare prices instantly), substitutes high (e-books, audiobooks, libraries, second-hand), rivalry high and suppliers medium
- The industry was hard for a general bookshop, but pressure was lower in niches where buyers had fewer substitutes and loyalty was higher

## Outcome

The owner specialised in local-interest titles with author events and a subscription, and stopped competing on price for bestsellers.

## Lessons

- Rating each force showed where the pressure was concentrated
- Positioning in a niche reduced the effect of several forces at once
- The analysis ended in a decision
