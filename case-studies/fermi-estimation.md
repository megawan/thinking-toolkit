# Case study: Sizing a coffee subscription market

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [Fermi Estimation](../methods/mental-models/fermi-estimation.md)

## Context

A founder wondered whether a coffee subscription in a city of one million adults was worth building, and had no market data.

## What was done

- Adults who drink coffee at home: about 60 percent, or 600,000
- Share who would pay for a subscription: 3 percent, or 18,000
- Spend: about $240 a year each, giving about $4.3 million a year
- A cautious case at 1 percent gave about $1.4 million

## Outcome

The founder judged that a market of one to four million dollars supported a small business but not a large investment, and ran a one-month pilot to measure the real subscription rate.

## Lessons

- The share who would subscribe was the assumption that mattered most
- A range was more honest than one number
- The estimate decided what to test, not whether to proceed blindly
