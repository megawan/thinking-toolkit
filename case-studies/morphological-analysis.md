# Case study: A food-delivery service for a small town

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [Morphological Analysis](../methods/ideation/morphological-analysis.md)

## Context

A group of residents wanted to start a local food-delivery service in a town with no delivery apps. Their first idea was a standard app and own drivers, which was too costly.

## What was done

- They defined five parameters: what is delivered, who delivers, how to order, when, and pricing, each with four options
- They traced several combinations: meal kits by subscription with own staff; groceries collected by customers; ready meals delivered by taxi drivers ordered by chat
- They removed combinations that did not work, such as on-demand delivery by volunteers

## Outcome

The taxi-driver combination had not been considered. It used spare capacity without hiring drivers, and the group tested it for a month in one neighbourhood.

## Lessons

- Listing every option per parameter exposed unconsidered choices
- Tracing odd combinations produced the best idea
- Evaluating a small shortlist kept the box useful
