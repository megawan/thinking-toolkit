# Case study: Ranking a product backlog

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [RICE and ICE Scoring](../methods/prioritisation/rice-and-ice-scoring.md)

## Context

A product team of six had four candidate features and a quarter of capacity. Opinions were split between the loudest requests.

## What was done

- The team estimated reach as users per quarter and effort in person-months, and agreed the impact and confidence scales
- RICE scores: faster signup 1,600, dark mode 1,000, saved searches 800, team accounts 250
- Team accounts had the highest impact per user but low confidence and high effort, so they ranked last
- They ran a small demand test for team accounts to raise confidence

## Outcome

The team built faster signup first and kept dark mode second. The demand test for team accounts came back strong, so the item was rescored upward for the next quarter.

## Lessons

- The confidence factor pushed the team to gather evidence
- Scores were treated as an input, not a verdict
- Dividing by effort favoured cheap, valuable items
