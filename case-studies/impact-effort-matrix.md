# Case study: Improving an online shop

> Illustrative scenario. It is a composite written to show the method in use, not a report about a real organisation.

Method: [Impact-Effort Matrix](../methods/prioritisation/impact-effort-matrix.md)

## Context

An online shop had more than a dozen improvement ideas and one developer. The team could not agree on an order.

## What was done

- Ideas were placed on a grid in a 30-minute session
- Showing delivery cost earlier in checkout and guest checkout landed in quick wins
- A site rebuild and a loyalty programme were major projects; a footer redesign was a fill-in; a custom home-page animation was a money pit
- The loyalty programme was reduced to a small pilot

## Outcome

The developer did the two quick wins first, ran the loyalty pilot, and dropped the animation. Checkout completion improved within weeks.

## Lessons

- Outliers (items placed far apart) exposed different assumptions
- Splitting a major project gave a cheap test
- The picture was revisited after the quick wins
