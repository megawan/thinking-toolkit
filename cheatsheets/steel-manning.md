# Steel-manning: cheat sheet

> State the other side's argument in its strongest, fairest form first.

Full guide: [Steel-manning](../methods/critical-thinking/steel-manning.md)

## Use it when

- Resolving a disagreement
- Reviewing a proposal you are inclined to reject
- Testing your own view for weaknesses

## Steps

1. Listen and ask questions until you understand
2. Identify the core claim, evidence and values
3. Restate it as strongly as you can
4. Ask: have I got it right, what am I missing?
5. List what you agree with and what you learned
6. Find the real disagreement (facts, values or priorities)
7. Respond to the strong version, not a weak one

## Key questions

- What is the strongest reason someone might hold this view?
- What evidence would support it?
- Under what conditions would it be right?
- What would convince me I am wrong?

## Watch out for

- A restatement they would not recognise
- Steel-manning only to dismiss
- Weaker steel mans of views you dislike
