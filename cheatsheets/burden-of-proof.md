# Burden of Proof: cheat sheet

> Whoever makes the claim has to support it.

Full guide: [Burden of Proof](../methods/critical-thinking/burden-of-proof.md)

## Use it when

- A claim is made without support
- A debate is stuck because each side demands the other prove them wrong
- Deciding how much evidence you need before acting

## Steps

1. Identify the claim and who makes it
2. Check the setting and the standard of proof
3. Scale the evidence to the claim: surprising or costly claims need more
4. Ask for the support
5. Judge whether it is relevant, sufficient and reliable
6. Notice burden shifting ('prove me wrong')
7. Decide what to do if the burden is not met: reserve judgment, take precautions, or reject
8. Support your own claims too

## Key questions

- Who is asserting what?
- What standard of proof fits this setting?
- What would count as enough?
- Am I holding my own view to the same standard?

## Watch out for

- 'Prove me wrong'
- Using absence of evidence as proof
- Applying the principle only to others
