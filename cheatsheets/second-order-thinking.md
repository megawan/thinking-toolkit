# Second-order Thinking: cheat sheet

> Ask 'and then what?' to see the consequences of the consequences.

Full guide: [Second-order Thinking](../methods/mental-models/second-order-thinking.md)

## Use it when

- Setting a rule, target, price or incentive
- A decision looks too good, with no visible cost
- A change many people will react to

## Steps

1. State the decision and its intended effect
2. List first-order effects
3. Ask 'and then what?' for each
4. List second-order effects, including other groups' reactions
5. Go one more step for the important ones
6. Look for feedback loops
7. Adjust the decision or add a safeguard
8. Choose indicators to watch

## Key questions

- And then what happens?
- Who will respond, and how?
- What behaviour does this reward?
- What happens if everyone does it?

## Watch out for

- Stopping at the first order
- Imagining endless chains
- Seeing only bad effects
