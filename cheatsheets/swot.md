# SWOT Analysis: cheat sheet

> Map strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats, then turn them into actions.

Full guide: [SWOT Analysis](../methods/strategy-and-risk/swot.md)

## Use it when

- Starting or reviewing a strategy or plan
- Deciding whether to enter a market, launch or take a role
- Aligning a team on where it stands

## Steps

1. Define the subject and the goal
2. Gather evidence
3. List Strengths and Weaknesses (internal)
4. List Opportunities and Threats (external)
5. Prioritise the few items that matter
6. Pair quadrants (TOWS) into actions
7. Assign owners and a review date

## Key questions

- What do we do better than others?
- Where do we lose or avoid?
- What outside change could help or hurt?
- How can a strength meet an opportunity?

## Watch out for

- Vague items ('good team')
- Mixing internal and external
- Stopping at the list
