# Cost-Benefit Analysis: cheat sheet

> Value every cost and benefit over time and see whether benefits win.

Full guide: [Cost-Benefit Analysis](../methods/decision-making/cost-benefit-analysis.md)

## Use it when

- A significant spend or commitment
- Several options to rank by value
- You must justify a choice to others

## Steps

1. Define options, horizon and a do-nothing baseline
2. List one-off and recurring costs, including hidden ones
3. List benefits without double counting
4. Put values on them (ranges where unsure)
5. Choose one discount rate for all options
6. Compute net benefit, NPV, BCR and payback
7. Test sensitivity and find the break-even
8. Add non-monetary factors and record assumptions

## Key questions

- What does it really cost, including running costs?
- What is the benefit, and is it counted once?
- At what value does the decision flip?

## Watch out for

- Forgetting recurring and hidden costs
- No discounting over long horizons
- Counting sunk costs
- False precision
