# Decision Journal: cheat sheet

> Write down what you decided, why and what you expect, then check later.

Full guide: [Decision Journal](../methods/reflection-and-learning/decision-journal.md)

## Use it when

- Significant decisions with uncertainty
- Decisions made repeatedly, so patterns can show
- When you want to calibrate your confidence

## Steps

1. Date the entry; describe the situation and the decision
2. List the options, including doing nothing
3. Record the choice and the main reasons
4. Note key assumptions and unknowns
5. State the expected outcome with a probability or range
6. Note your state of mind
7. Set a review date and signals
8. At review: compare, separate skill from luck, write a lesson

## Key questions

- What do I expect, and how sure am I?
- What would show me I was wrong?
- Was the reasoning sound given what I knew?
- Would I decide the same way again?

## Watch out for

- Writing after the outcome is known
- Vague entries
- Judging by outcome alone
- Never reviewing
