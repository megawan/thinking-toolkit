# Five Whys: cheat sheet

> Ask why until you reach a cause you can fix.

Full guide: [Five Whys](../methods/root-cause/five-whys.md)

## Use it when

- A problem keeps coming back
- A failure needs a lasting fix, not a patch
- The cause chain looks fairly linear

## Steps

1. Write the problem as a specific, observable fact
2. Ask why it happened; answer with something you can verify
3. Ask why about that answer
4. Repeat until you reach a process, system or decision
5. Read the chain backwards with 'therefore' to test it
6. Define actions with owners and dates

## Key questions

- Why did this happen? How do we know?
- What allowed that to happen or go unnoticed?
- If we fix this cause, does the problem go away?

## Watch out for

- Stopping at a person ('Sam forgot')
- Answering from assumption instead of evidence
- Following one path when there are several causes
