# User Journey Map: cheat sheet

> Lay out what a person does, thinks and feels step by step.

Full guide: [User Journey Map](../methods/design-and-product/user-journey-map.md)

## Use it when

- Improving an end-to-end experience
- Several teams touch the same customer and do not see the whole
- Drop-offs or complaints suggest a problem but not where

## Steps

1. Choose one persona and one scenario
2. Set where the journey starts and ends
3. Gather evidence: interviews, analytics, support data
4. List stages in order
5. Add actions, touchpoints, thoughts and an emotion line
6. Mark pain points and moments of delight
7. Find opportunities and prioritise them
8. Share, assign owners, update after changes

## Key questions

- What does the person do at this stage?
- Where do they meet us?
- How do they feel, and why?
- Where does the experience break down?

## Watch out for

- Building from assumptions
- Too broad a scope
- A poster that is never used
