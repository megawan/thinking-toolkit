# Crazy 8s: cheat sheet

> Eight different sketches in eight minutes, one per minute.

Full guide: [Crazy 8s](../methods/ideation/crazy-8s.md)

## Use it when

- A design or product problem with a first idea you doubt
- A team needs several concrete alternatives in minutes
- A warm-up before a longer design session

## Steps

1. State the problem as one question
2. Fold a sheet into 8 panels
3. Set an 8-minute timer with a signal every minute
4. Sketch one different idea per minute
5. Stop at the timer; do not refine
6. Share each sheet in 30-60 seconds without debate
7. Dot-vote and pick one or two ideas to develop

## Key questions

- What is a completely different way to do this?
- What would the opposite look like?
- What is simpler, cheaper, or for a different user?

## Watch out for

- Making all eight ideas the same
- Polishing instead of sketching
- Debating before everyone has shared
