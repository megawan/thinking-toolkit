# Logical Fallacies: cheat sheet

> Learn the common ways reasoning fails while sounding convincing.

Full guide: [Logical Fallacies](../methods/critical-thinking/logical-fallacies.md)

## Use it when

- Reading a proposal, report or news story
- Preparing or reviewing your own argument
- A decision rests on a persuasive but thin case

## Steps

1. Restate the argument: conclusion and premises
2. Check relevance of each premise
3. Check support: true? evidence? strong link?
4. Look for gaps and hidden assumptions
5. If a pattern fits, name the flaw in plain words
6. Respond fairly: restate their best case, point to the gap, ask for what is missing
7. Keep the conclusion open (the fallacy fallacy)
8. Check your own argument against the same list

## Key questions

- What exactly is the conclusion, and what supports it?
- Is this relevant to the claim?
- What is assumed but not stated?
- Could the conclusion still be true for other reasons?

## Watch out for

- Labelling instead of explaining
- The fallacy fallacy
- Seeing fallacies only in others
