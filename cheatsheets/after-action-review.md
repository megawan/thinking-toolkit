# After Action Review: cheat sheet

> Right after an event, compare what was supposed to happen with what did, and learn why.

Full guide: [After Action Review](../methods/reflection-and-learning/after-action-review.md)

## Use it when

- After a milestone, launch, presentation, exercise or incident
- After a routine task done by a team
- Whenever you want a fast learning loop

## Steps

1. Hold it soon, while memory is fresh
2. Invite everyone who did the work
3. State the ground rules: learning, not blame
4. What did we expect to happen?
5. What actually happened? (facts, timeline)
6. Why was there a difference? (go below the first answer)
7. What can we learn, and what will we sustain and improve?
8. Record the lessons and share them

## Key questions

- What was supposed to happen?
- What actually happened?
- Why the difference?
- What will we do differently next time?

## Watch out for

- Waiting too long
- Blame
- Only reviewing failures
- Vague lessons ('communicate better')
