# OODA Loop: cheat sheet

> Observe, orient, decide and act, then repeat faster than the situation changes.

Full guide: [OODA Loop](../methods/decision-making/ooda-loop.md)

## Use it when

- The situation changes quickly
- You can take small actions and learn from the result
- A team is stuck analysing and not acting

## Steps

1. Observe: collect fresh facts, note what you cannot see
2. Orient: interpret them, challenge assumptions, update your picture
3. Decide: choose the next small, reversible action
4. Act promptly
5. Observe the result and start the next loop
6. Shorten delays between stages
7. After each loop, update assumptions and playbooks

## Key questions

- What is happening right now?
- What does it mean, and what has changed?
- What is the smallest useful next step?
- What did that action change?

## Watch out for

- Speed without orientation
- Getting stuck in orient
- Acting without checking the result
