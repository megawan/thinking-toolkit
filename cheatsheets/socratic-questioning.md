# Socratic Questioning: cheat sheet

> Examine a claim by asking disciplined questions.

Full guide: [Socratic Questioning](../methods/critical-thinking/socratic-questioning.md)

## Use it when

- Reviewing a plan, claim or decision
- Coaching or teaching
- Examining your own beliefs

## Steps

1. Set the tone: shared inquiry, not a trap
2. Clarify the claim
3. Probe assumptions, one at a time
4. Ask for reasons and evidence
5. Explore other viewpoints
6. Follow the implications and consequences
7. Question the question
8. Summarise in their words and agree next steps

## Key questions

- What do you mean by...?
- What are you assuming?
- How do you know?
- How would someone who disagrees see it?
- If that were true, what follows?
- Why is this the right question?

## Watch out for

- Leading questions
- Too many questions at once
- An aggressive tone
- Never concluding
