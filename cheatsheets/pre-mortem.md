# Pre-mortem: cheat sheet

> Imagine the project has already failed, then find out why while the plan can still change.

Full guide: [Pre-mortem](../methods/strategy-and-risk/pre-mortem.md)

## Use it when

- At the kickoff of a project or launch
- Before an expensive or hard-to-reverse decision
- When the team is very confident and nobody raises risks

## Steps

1. Summarise the plan, goal and date
2. Say: 'It is a year from now and the project failed. Why?'
3. Everyone writes reasons silently and alone
4. Share one reason at a time, go around the room
5. Group into themes and rate likelihood and impact
6. Plan for top risks: prevent, reduce, prepare or accept
7. Update the plan with owners and dates

## Key questions

- Who would have to let us down for this to fail?
- Which assumption, if wrong, sinks us?
- What did we ignore because it was uncomfortable?

## Watch out for

- Seniors speaking first
- Vague reasons
- No actions with owners
- Doing it too late
