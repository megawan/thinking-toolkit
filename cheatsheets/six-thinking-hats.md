# Six Thinking Hats: cheat sheet

> Look at a topic from six separate angles, one at a time.

Full guide: [Six Thinking Hats](../methods/decision-making/six-thinking-hats.md)

## Use it when

- A group argues across each other
- A decision needs balanced consideration
- One voice dominates the room

## Steps

1. State the topic and choose a facilitator (Blue)
2. Pick a hat sequence and 3-5 minutes per hat
3. White: facts and gaps
4. Red: feelings, no justification needed
5. Black: risks and weaknesses
6. Yellow: benefits and value
7. Green: new ideas and alternatives
8. Blue: summarise, decide, agree next steps

## Key questions

- White: what do we know and not know?
- Red: what is the gut reaction?
- Black: what could go wrong?
- Yellow: what is the upside?
- Green: what else could we do?
- Blue: what have we decided?

## Watch out for

- Mixing hats; park off-hat comments
- Skipping Black or Yellow
- Ending without a decision
