# Pareto Analysis: cheat sheet

> A few causes usually produce most of the effect; find them first.

Full guide: [Pareto Analysis](../methods/prioritisation/pareto-analysis.md)

## Use it when

- Many causes, complaints or tasks compete for attention
- You have counts or costs
- You need to justify focusing on a few items

## Steps

1. Define what you measure and the period
2. List consistent, non-overlapping categories
3. Collect and total the data
4. Sort from largest to smallest
5. Compute share and cumulative percent
6. Chart bars with a cumulative line
7. Act on the vital few, then re-measure

## Key questions

- What are we counting, and over what period?
- Which categories make up about 70-80 percent?
- Is a small but severe item being overlooked?

## Watch out for

- Poor or inconsistent data
- A large 'other' bucket
- Ignoring severity
- Doing it once only
