# Empathy Map: cheat sheet

> Put what a person says, thinks, does and feels on one page.

Full guide: [Empathy Map](../methods/design-and-product/empathy-map.md)

## Use it when

- After interviews or observation, to synthesise findings
- A team debates what users want without evidence
- Sharing user understanding with people who did not do the research

## Steps

1. Choose one user segment
2. Gather evidence: interviews, observation, tickets
3. Fill Says and Does from direct evidence
4. Infer Thinks and Feels, and mark them as inferences
5. Add Sees, Hears, Pains and Gains if used
6. Look for tensions between says and does
7. Write insights and open questions

## Key questions

- What did they tell us, and what did we see them do?
- What might they be thinking but not saying?
- Where do words and actions differ?
- What do we still not know?

## Watch out for

- Filling it with assumptions
- Mixing user types
- Stopping at description
