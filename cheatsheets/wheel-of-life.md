# Wheel of Life: cheat sheet

> Rate your satisfaction in each area of life and see where attention is needed.

Full guide: [Wheel of Life](../methods/reflection-and-learning/wheel-of-life.md)

## Use it when

- The start of a year, new phase or transition
- Something feels off but you cannot say what
- Before setting goals or priorities

## Steps

1. Draw a circle with 8 segments (or your own areas)
2. Name the areas to fit your life
3. Score satisfaction 1-10 for each, by first feeling
4. Look at the shape: which areas are low or high?
5. Ask what is behind the low scores and which areas matter now
6. Notice connections between areas
7. Choose one or two focus areas
8. Set one small action each; repeat in three to six months

## Key questions

- What is working well here, and why?
- What would make this score one point higher?
- Is a low score a problem, or a choice for this stage of life?
- Which area, if improved, would lift others?

## Watch out for

- Overthinking the numbers
- Aiming for a perfect circle
- Too many goals at once
