# Value Proposition Canvas: cheat sheet

> Describe the customer's jobs, pains and gains, and show how your offer fits.

Full guide: [Value Proposition Canvas](../methods/design-and-product/value-proposition-canvas.md)

## Use it when

- Designing a new product or service
- A product is not selling and you want to find out why
- Refining a message for a customer segment

## Steps

1. Choose one customer segment
2. Fill the customer profile from research: jobs, pains, gains
3. Rank them by importance
4. Fill the value map: products and services, pain relievers, gain creators
5. Check the fit: link the top pains and gains to relievers and creators
6. Note gaps and features that address nothing
7. Mark the riskiest assumptions and test them

## Key questions

- What is the customer trying to get done?
- What are their top pains and gains?
- Which feature relieves which pain?
- What is our least certain assumption?

## Watch out for

- Describing features instead of benefits
- Assuming instead of researching
- Several segments on one canvas
