# Brainwriting 6-3-5: cheat sheet

> Six people write three ideas in five minutes, pass the sheet and build on each other in silence.

Full guide: [Brainwriting 6-3-5](../methods/ideation/brainwriting-635.md)

## Use it when

- A group where a few people dominate
- You want many ideas fast
- People hold back ideas out loud

## Steps

1. Frame the question (a 'How might we' question works well)
2. Give each person a sheet with 3 columns and 6 rows
3. Round 1 (5 min): write 3 ideas in the first row
4. Pass the sheet to the next person
5. Read the row above, then add 3 new or developed ideas
6. Repeat until every sheet has passed through everyone
7. Cluster, merge duplicates and select
8. Agree next steps and owners

## Key questions

- What new angle can I add to the idea above?
- How could this idea be bigger, cheaper or simpler?
- Which ideas group together?

## Watch out for

- Talking during rounds
- Copying instead of building on ideas
- Stopping after generation without choosing
