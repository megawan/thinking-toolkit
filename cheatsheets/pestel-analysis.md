# PESTEL Analysis: cheat sheet

> Scan the political, economic, social, technological, environmental and legal forces outside your organisation.

Full guide: [PESTEL Analysis](../methods/strategy-and-risk/pestel-analysis.md)

## Use it when

- Entering a new market or country
- Starting a strategy or planning cycle
- Assessing external risks to a business or project

## Steps

1. Set the scope, region and time horizon
2. Brainstorm factors under P, E, S, T, E, L
3. Research the important ones with data and sources
4. Rate impact and certainty; mark opportunity or threat
5. Prioritise the few that matter
6. Look for links between factors
7. Decide actions and set indicators and a review date

## Key questions

- Which policy, price or social change could affect us most?
- What technology could change our costs or customers?
- What laws and standards must we meet, and what is changing?
- What would we watch to know it is happening?

## Watch out for

- A long list with no priority
- Generic entries
- No evidence or follow-up
