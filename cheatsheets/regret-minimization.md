# Regret Minimization: cheat sheet

> Imagine yourself at 80 looking back, and choose the path you would regret least.

Full guide: [Regret Minimization](../methods/decision-making/regret-minimization.md)

## Use it when

- A major life or career choice
- Pros and cons are balanced
- You feel pulled to play safe and want to test it

## Steps

1. State the choice and realistic options, including staying put
2. Pick a distant point (age 80, end of career)
3. Look back at each option and write what you feel
4. Compare regret of action and regret of inaction
5. Check which option fits your values
6. Face the worst realistic case; limit risk where possible
7. Decide and plan the first step

## Key questions

- Would I regret trying and failing, or never trying?
- What will I wish I had at least tried?
- Is this fear or a lasting wish?
- What would I tell a friend in my position?

## Watch out for

- Using it to justify impulse
- Imagining only good outcomes
- Ignoring responsibilities to others
