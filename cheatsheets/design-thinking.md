# Design Thinking: cheat sheet

> Understand people, try rough ideas fast, learn from their reactions.

Full guide: [Design Thinking](../methods/design-and-product/design-thinking.md)

## Use it when

- The problem is unclear or about human behaviour
- Existing solutions are not working and you do not know why
- A team needs shared understanding of users

## Steps

1. Frame the challenge: who is it for, and what area?
2. Empathise: talk to and observe 5-10 people
3. Define: insights, point-of-view statement, How Might We question
4. Ideate: many ideas first, then select
5. Prototype: the cheapest version that answers a question
6. Test with real users; watch and ask why
7. Iterate and loop back to any stage

## Key questions

- Who are we designing for, and what do they need?
- What did we observe that surprised us?
- What is the cheapest way to test this idea?
- What did users do, not just say?

## Watch out for

- Workshop theatre without real users
- Skipping empathy to get to ideas
- Polished prototypes too early
