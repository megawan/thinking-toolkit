# Lateral Thinking: cheat sheet

> Step sideways to find solutions logic alone would not reach.

Full guide: [Lateral Thinking](../methods/ideation/lateral-thinking.md)

## Use it when

- Obvious solutions have failed
- You need many options before choosing
- The way the problem is framed may be the limit

## Steps

1. State the problem in one sentence
2. Pick a technique: challenge assumptions, random input, provocation (PO), reversal, alternatives or reframing
3. Generate ideas without judging
4. Switch technique and repeat
5. Look for the useful core in odd ideas
6. Evaluate and test a shortlist cheaply

## Key questions

- What are we assuming, and what if it were false?
- What if we did the exact opposite?
- What would a person from another field do?

## Watch out for

- Judging ideas too early
- Treating a provocation as the answer
- Stopping at the first good idea
