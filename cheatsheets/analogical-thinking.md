# Analogical Thinking: cheat sheet

> Borrow a solution from a different field by asking what your problem is like.

Full guide: [Analogical Thinking](../methods/ideation/analogical-thinking.md)

## Use it when

- Standard solutions in your field have not worked
- You want an unfamiliar angle
- A group's thinking is too uniform

## Steps

1. State the problem and the function you need
2. Restate it in general, field-free terms
3. List places where this general problem also exists
4. Study how the best matches solve it
5. Map each solution back to your context
6. Note where the analogy breaks
7. Test a small version

## Key questions

- Where else does this same problem exist?
- How does nature or another industry solve it?
- What corresponds to what, and where does it not match?

## Watch out for

- Surface analogies with a different structure
- Forcing a weak fit
- Staying inside your own field
