# Fermi Estimation: cheat sheet

> Break an unknown into small guesses and combine them for a close-enough number.

Full guide: [Fermi Estimation](../methods/mental-models/fermi-estimation.md)

## Use it when

- Getting a rough size before a detailed analysis
- Checking whether a claim is plausible
- Sizing a market, workload or budget

## Steps

1. State the question with a unit
2. Break it into factors: quantity = A x B x C
3. Estimate each factor with round numbers
4. Anchor on known figures (population, hours, prices)
5. Calculate step by step
6. Sanity-check with a second route
7. State a range and the assumption that matters most

## Key questions

- What is the unit of the answer?
- What factors multiply to give it?
- Which factor am I least sure about?
- Does a second route give the same order of magnitude?

## Watch out for

- False precision
- Hidden assumptions
- Forgetting units (per day, per year, per person)
