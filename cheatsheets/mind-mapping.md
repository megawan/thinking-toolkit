# Mind Mapping: cheat sheet

> Branch outward from a central topic to see ideas and their links on one page.

Full guide: [Mind Mapping](../methods/ideation/mind-mapping.md)

## Use it when

- Exploring a topic before deciding what to do
- A brainstorm needs structure
- Planning a talk, document or project

## Steps

1. Write the central topic in the middle
2. Draw main branches for the major themes
3. Add sub-branches with keywords, not sentences
4. Keep going outward without judging
5. Draw cross-links between branches
6. Use colour or icons to group themes
7. Review thin branches, then choose next steps

## Key questions

- What are the main themes?
- What else connects to this?
- Which branch is thin or missing?

## Watch out for

- Writing sentences instead of keywords
- A map that is only a list (no cross-links)
- No outcome after the map
