# Impact-Effort Matrix: cheat sheet

> Plot ideas by impact and effort to find quick wins and avoid money pits.

Full guide: [Impact-Effort Matrix](../methods/prioritisation/impact-effort-matrix.md)

## Use it when

- A group needs a shared view of a backlog
- You want quick wins for momentum
- A first rough sort before a deeper method

## Steps

1. Define the goal that impact is measured against
2. List items, one per sticky note
3. Agree what high and low mean for impact and effort
4. Place each item on the grid, then discuss outliers
5. Do quick wins first, choose major projects deliberately
6. Use fill-ins when there is slack; drop the money pits
7. Try to split major projects or simplify them

## Key questions

- Impact on what goal?
- Does effort include testing, support and maintenance?
- Can a major project be split into a quick win?

## Watch out for

- Vague impact with no goal
- Underestimating effort
- Doing only quick wins
