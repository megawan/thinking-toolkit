# 10-10-10: cheat sheet

> Ask how you will feel about it in 10 minutes, 10 months and 10 years.

Full guide: [10-10-10](../methods/decision-making/ten-ten-ten.md)

## Use it when

- You are about to react in the heat of the moment
- Short-term pull conflicts with a long-term goal
- A quick check before a bigger analysis

## Steps

1. State the decision and options, including doing nothing
2. For each option, answer for 10 minutes, 10 months and 10 years
3. Keep each answer to a line or two
4. Compare where the options flip
5. Weigh how much each horizon matters here
6. Choose and plan for any short-term cost

## Key questions

- How will I feel in 10 minutes? In 10 months? In 10 years?
- Which horizon matters most for this decision?
- What does doing nothing lead to?

## Watch out for

- Assuming the short term is always wrong
- Wild guesses about 10 years
- Skipping the middle horizon
