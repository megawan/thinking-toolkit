# Business Model and Lean Canvas: cheat sheet

> Describe a whole business on one page with nine blocks.

Full guide: [Business Model and Lean Canvas](../methods/strategy-and-risk/business-model-and-lean-canvas.md)

## Use it when

- Starting a new product, service or business
- Explaining or analysing a business
- Planning a change to a business model

## Steps

1. Pick the canvas: Lean Canvas for early ideas, Business Model Canvas for mature ones
2. Start with customer segment and the problem or value
3. Fill the other blocks in short phrases; mark guesses
4. Check coherence: do the blocks support each other, do revenues cover costs?
5. Rank the riskiest assumptions
6. Test the riskiest first; update the canvas after each test

## Key questions

- Who has the problem, and how do they solve it today?
- Why is our offer different and worth paying for?
- Which assumption would sink the model if wrong?
- Which number tells us it is working?

## Watch out for

- Treating guesses as facts
- Too many segments at once
- Never updating the canvas
