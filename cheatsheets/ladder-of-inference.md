# Ladder of Inference: cheat sheet

> Trace how you move from facts to conclusions and actions.

Full guide: [Ladder of Inference](../methods/critical-thinking/ladder-of-inference.md)

## Use it when

- You feel certain about someone's motives
- Two people see the same events and conclude differently
- A conflict feels stuck

## Steps

1. Notice your conclusion and how sure you are
2. Walk down: belief, assumption, meaning, selected data
3. Return to the observable data (what a camera would show)
4. Ask what data you did not select
5. Mark assumptions as fact or guess
6. List at least two other interpretations
7. Check with the other person using inquiry
8. Choose the action after the check

## Key questions

- What exactly did I see and hear?
- What else could explain it?
- Which of my assumptions are guesses?
- What did the other person see?

## Watch out for

- Analysing others before yourself
- Skipping the observable data
- Stopping at understanding without deciding what to do
