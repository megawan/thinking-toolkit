# Cognitive Biases: cheat sheet

> Know the common ways judgment goes wrong, and build checks that counter them.

Full guide: [Cognitive Biases](../methods/critical-thinking/cognitive-biases.md)

## Use it when

- Before a major decision, as a final check
- Reviewing a forecast, estimate or plan
- Understanding why a call went wrong

## Steps

1. Identify the decision and where judgment mattered
2. Go through the list of biases and ask whether each could be at work
3. Mark the likely ones using signs (early anchor, only supportive evidence, money already spent)
4. Pick a countermeasure for each
5. Change the process: add a step, a person or a rule
6. Record the decision and the reasons
7. Review the outcome later

## Key questions

- What would prove me wrong?
- Did I estimate before seeing others' numbers?
- Am I counting money already spent?
- How did similar cases turn out?

## Watch out for

- Using bias labels to win arguments
- Believing you are immune
- Not changing the process
