# Jobs To Be Done: cheat sheet

> People hire products to make progress; find the job, and you find what to build.

Full guide: [Jobs To Be Done](../methods/design-and-product/jobs-to-be-done.md)

## Use it when

- Deciding what to build or improve
- Understanding why customers choose, switch or leave
- Finding competitors outside your category

## Steps

1. Pick whom to interview: recent buyers, switchers, people who did not buy
2. Ask for the story: first thought, trigger, search, options, decision, first use
3. Probe the situation and the struggle
4. Probe the four forces: push, pull, anxiety, habit
5. Write the job: When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [outcome]
6. Group similar jobs; list the alternatives, including doing nothing
7. Find under-served jobs and turn them into decisions

## Key questions

- What was happening when you first thought about this?
- What were you using before? What was not working?
- What else did you consider, including doing nothing?
- What almost stopped you?

## Watch out for

- Confusing the job with the product
- Asking what people want instead of what happened
- Ignoring emotional and social jobs
