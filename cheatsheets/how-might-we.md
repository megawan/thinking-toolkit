# How Might We: cheat sheet

> Turn a problem into an open, hopeful question.

Full guide: [How Might We](../methods/ideation/how-might-we.md)

## Use it when

- At the start of a brainstorm or design project
- After research, to turn findings into opportunities
- A team is stuck complaining about a problem

## Steps

1. Gather insights about the problem and the people affected
2. Spot the tensions and unmet needs
3. Write at least five 'How might we...?' questions per insight
4. Vary the scope: broad and narrow
5. Test each question: no hidden solution, many possible answers
6. Cluster and choose one to three questions
7. Take them into an idea session

## Key questions

- What is the need behind the complaint?
- Does the question hide a solution?
- Is it broad enough for many answers, and narrow enough to start?

## Watch out for

- A solution hidden in the question
- Too broad or too narrow
- Negative or blaming framing
