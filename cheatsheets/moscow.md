# MoSCoW: cheat sheet

> Sort requirements into Must, Should, Could and Won't.

Full guide: [MoSCoW](../methods/prioritisation/moscow.md)

## Use it when

- A project has a fixed date or budget
- Stakeholders disagree about scope
- Everything in the backlog is labelled urgent

## Steps

1. State the goal, the date and the capacity
2. List candidate requirements as clear sentences
3. Agree what 'Must' means for this project
4. Sort each item and challenge every Must
5. Estimate effort per item
6. Keep Musts within about 60 percent of capacity
7. Record the Won'ts and why
8. Drop Coulds first, then Shoulds, if estimates grow

## Key questions

- What happens if this is missing on launch day?
- Is there a workaround?
- Could it follow within a few weeks?
- Is it required by law, safety or contract?

## Watch out for

- Too many Musts
- No capacity check
- Different meanings of 'Must'
- Hiding the Won'ts
