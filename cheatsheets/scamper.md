# SCAMPER: cheat sheet

> Run an existing idea through seven prompts to change it.

Full guide: [SCAMPER](../methods/ideation/scamper.md)

## Use it when

- A product, service or process works but could be better
- Brainstorming has stalled
- You want structured variations quickly

## Steps

1. Name the subject and why it exists
2. Go through the verbs one at a time, 3-5 minutes each: Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to another use, Eliminate, Reverse
3. Write several answers per verb
4. Mark the promising ideas
5. Combine and shortlist, then test

## Key questions

- What can be replaced? What can be merged?
- What can be borrowed from elsewhere?
- What can be removed without losing the core value?
- What if the order or roles were flipped?

## Watch out for

- Skipping the uncomfortable verbs (Eliminate, Reverse)
- Judging while generating
- Losing sight of the core purpose
