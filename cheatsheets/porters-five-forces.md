# Porter's Five Forces: cheat sheet

> Judge an industry by five pressures on its profits.

Full guide: [Porter's Five Forces](../methods/strategy-and-risk/porters-five-forces.md)

## Use it when

- Deciding whether to enter or invest in an industry
- Reviewing strategy and positioning
- Understanding why profits in a sector are thin

## Steps

1. Define the industry and its scope
2. Analyse each force: new entrants, suppliers, buyers, substitutes, rivalry
3. Rate each low, medium or high, with reasons
4. Identify the strongest forces and trends
5. Look for positioning that reduces the pressure
6. Decide actions and review when the industry changes

## Key questions

- How easy is it for newcomers to enter?
- Who can raise prices or demand more: suppliers or buyers?
- What else could customers use instead?
- How intense is rivalry?

## Watch out for

- A list with no judgment
- Too broad an industry
- No positioning decision at the end
