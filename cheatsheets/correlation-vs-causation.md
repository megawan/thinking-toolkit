# Correlation vs Causation: cheat sheet

> Two things moving together does not mean one causes the other.

Full guide: [Correlation vs Causation](../methods/critical-thinking/correlation-vs-causation.md)

## Use it when

- A report claims that X leads to Y
- Deciding whether to roll out a change after a trend
- Designing a test to find what really works

## Steps

1. State the claim: X causes Y
2. Check timing: did X come first?
3. Consider reverse causation
4. Look for a third factor that affects both
5. Consider chance: sample size and number of comparisons
6. Check who is compared; look for a comparison group
7. Prefer an experiment (randomised comparison)
8. Weigh wider evidence and word the conclusion to match it

## Key questions

- Did X come before Y?
- Could Y cause X?
- What else could affect both?
- What happened where X did not occur?

## Watch out for

- Post hoc reasoning
- Ignoring confounders
- Cherry-picking correlations
