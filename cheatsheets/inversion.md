# Inversion: cheat sheet

> Ask how to fail, then avoid exactly that.

Full guide: [Inversion](../methods/mental-models/inversion.md)

## Use it when

- A forward plan feels vague or stuck
- You want to find risks early
- A group is too optimistic

## Steps

1. State the goal
2. Invert it: how could we guarantee failure?
3. List specific ways to fail, honestly and ruthlessly
4. Flip each into a rule or action
5. Check what you are already doing from the list
6. Add positive steps that inversion alone does not give

## Key questions

- What would guarantee this fails?
- Which of these are we doing right now?
- What is the opposite of the belief we hold?

## Watch out for

- Stopping at 'avoid' without positive steps
- Vague failure causes
- Slipping into pessimism
