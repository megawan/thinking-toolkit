# Double Diamond: cheat sheet

> Open up and narrow down twice: first the right problem, then the right solution.

Full guide: [Double Diamond](../methods/design-and-product/double-diamond.md)

## Use it when

- Planning how a project will work
- A team keeps jumping to solutions
- A team keeps exploring and never decides

## Steps

1. Discover (diverge): research widely, no solutions yet
2. Define (converge): make sense of it and choose the problem; write the brief
3. Develop (diverge): many solutions, rough prototypes
4. Deliver (converge): test, refine, launch
5. Loop back if testing shows the problem was wrong
6. Set a time box and a way to choose for each phase

## Key questions

- Are we opening up or narrowing down right now?
- Are we solving the right problem?
- What would make us go back a stage?

## Watch out for

- Skipping the first diamond
- Diverging forever
- Treating it as a rigid timetable
