# Red Team and Devil's Advocate: cheat sheet

> Give someone the job of attacking the plan before reality does.

Full guide: [Red Team and Devil's Advocate](../methods/critical-thinking/red-team-and-devils-advocate.md)

## Use it when

- A significant decision that is hard to reverse
- The team is very confident or in strong agreement
- A field with adversaries, such as security or competition

## Steps

1. Define the plan and the question
2. Choose the role: one devil's advocate or a separate red team
3. Pick challengers independent of the plan's authors; rotate the role
4. Set rules: challenge ideas, not people
5. Ask how to defeat the plan and what the weakest assumption is
6. Record findings with severity and likelihood
7. Owners respond: fix, mitigate, accept or reject, with reasons
8. Follow up on agreed changes

## Key questions

- What must be true for this to work, and what is least likely?
- How would a determined opponent beat it?
- What happens if a key person, supplier or customer leaves?
- What are we ignoring because it is unpleasant?

## Watch out for

- Ceremonial challenge with the decision already made
- A defensive owner
- No follow-up on findings
