# Retrospective: cheat sheet

> Look back at the work at regular intervals and agree on a few changes.

Full guide: [Retrospective](../methods/reflection-and-learning/retrospective.md)

## Use it when

- The end of a sprint, project phase or release
- After a difficult period or incident
- A team's work does not feel as smooth as it could

## Steps

1. Prepare: choose the period, facilitator, format and facts
2. Set the stage: goal, prime directive, confidentiality
3. Gather data silently (Start / Stop / Continue, 4Ls, Mad / Sad / Glad)
4. Group notes into themes
5. Generate insights: why, patterns, what is in our control
6. Vote for one to three themes
7. Decide specific actions with owners and dates
8. Close, and open the next session with these actions

## Key questions

- What went well? What did not?
- Why did that happen?
- What is in our control?
- What is the smallest change we can try next period?

## Watch out for

- Complaints without actions
- Too many actions
- Blame, or a manager who dominates
- No follow-up
