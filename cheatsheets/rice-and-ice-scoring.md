# RICE and ICE Scoring: cheat sheet

> Score ideas by reach, impact, confidence and effort to rank a backlog.

Full guide: [RICE and ICE Scoring](../methods/prioritisation/rice-and-ice-scoring.md)

## Use it when

- A long backlog needs ordering
- Stakeholders disagree on priority
- You want a record of why something was chosen

## Steps

1. List items clearly
2. Choose RICE (numbers available) or ICE (quick, 1-10 scales)
3. Define the scales before scoring
4. Score each factor, alone first if in a group
5. RICE = Reach x Impact x Confidence / Effort; ICE = Impact x Confidence x Ease
6. Sort and sanity-check the order
7. Check dependencies and hard constraints
8. Revisit as data arrives

## Key questions

- How many people or events does this reach in a period?
- How much does it change things for each of them?
- How sure are we, and what is the evidence?
- How much work is it, in total?

## Watch out for

- False precision (800 vs 780 is a tie)
- Adjusting inputs until a favourite wins
- Inconsistent scales
